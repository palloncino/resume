# Walk-in outreach playbook — Malta

**Internal only.** Last updated: July 2026.

---

## Strategy

Walk-ins are the correct first move. Target:

- Letting branches
- Property management teams
- Relocation companies
- Short-let managers

**Not** random sales-only agents.

**Daily target:** 6 walk-ins per day (not 20).

**Single outcome per visit:**

> “Can I send you my sample video report on WhatsApp?”

Once they say yes → warm contact. Follow up next morning:

> “Good morning, this is Antonio from Property Operations Malta. As mentioned, here is the sample video inspection/report. If you ever need quick property checks, key handovers or contractor verification, I can help on short notice.”

---

## Best hours

Go **Tuesday–Thursday**.

| Window | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **10:30–12:00** | Settled, not yet at lunch |
| **14:30–16:30** | Managers / letting agents after viewings |

**Avoid:**

| Window | Why |
|--------|-----|
| 09:00–10:00 | Opening, planning, replying |
| 12:30–14:00 | Lunch / dead zone |
| After 17:00 | Viewings or done for the day |
| Monday morning | Backlog |
| Friday afternoon | Low yield unless you know them |

---

## What to say at the door

Do **not** say “I built a website.”

> “Hi, I’m Antonio. I live in Malta and I’m starting a small on-the-ground property inspection service. I help agencies and overseas landlords with site visits, photo/video reports, key handovers and contractor checks. I’m not here to take clients from you — I can be useful when your team is overloaded or when an owner needs visual proof. Who handles rentals or property management here?”

Then show the **60-second demo**.

- [ ] Simple one-page PDF or flyer
- [ ] WhatsApp ready
